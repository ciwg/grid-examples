# Workflow and evidence guide

Any member may record an observation and potential safety hold. Only a
recognized area steward may clear a hold after an inspection. Off-site loans
retain the borrower, deadline, and accepted area-policy snapshot; returns add
condition evidence. Legacy loans without a historical policy snapshot remain
visible as incomplete evidence and must not be presented with inferred terms.
Source: DI-dapod; DI-patag; DI-malih; DI-lasif.
